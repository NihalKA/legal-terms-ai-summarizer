✅ Before You Sign - Questions to Ask

  • What is the exact interest rate? Can it change?
  • What are ALL the fees I'll pay (processing, annual, prepayment)?
  • What happens if I miss 2-3 payments?
  • Can you terminate the loan? Under what conditions?
  • What property/assets are at risk if I can't repay?
  • Can you change terms without my consent?
  • Is there a grace period for late payments?
  • Can I prepay without penalty?
